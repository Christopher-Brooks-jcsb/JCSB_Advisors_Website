The practice serves individuals, families, and business owners navigating the full arc of their financial lives — building wealth, protecting it, distributing it efficiently, and leaving a lasting legacy. **Always reference Brent Turner, CFP® on first mention.** The CFP® designation is a meaningful differentiator. He is an advisor and a credentialed professional — never a "representative," a "salesman," or a generic "financial advisor." ### Core Identity: "The Advisor, Not the Order-Taker" JCSB is not a product vendor. The practice is rooted in four principles: - **Holistic Planning** — Every piece of the financial picture: income, investments, insurance, taxes, estate, and goals. - **Independent Guidance** — We work for the client. Not for a fund company, a brokerage, or an insurance carrier. - **Proactive Relationship** — Clients receive regular reviews, personalized reporting, and a named point of contact. This is not a "set it and forget it" practice. - **Transparent Compensation** — Primarily fee-based (AUM fees), aligning our interests with growing and protecting client wealth. In limited circumstances, commission-based products may be appropriate — when they are, we explain why. ### Mission To give individuals, families, and business owners **"Safe of Mind"** — the confidence that their financial plan is documented, actively monitored, and tailored to their life — not assembled from generic products. ### What Sets JCSB Apart - A CFP®-credentialed lead advisor, not a sales representative - Professional planning software with real reports and visualizations - Institutional-grade investment oversight scaled for individuals and small businesses - Coordination across retirement, insurance, tax efficiency, and estate planning - Independence — no allegiance to any recordkeeper, insurance carrier, or fund company --- ## Part 2: The Ideal Client JCSB serves individuals, families, and business owners with meaningful assets to protect, grow, and transfer. Fit is based on complexity and relationship potential, not a strict asset minimum. **Common profiles:** - Business owners building personal wealth alongside their company - Pre-retirees (ages 50–65) transitioning from accumulation to income - Executives with equity comp, deferred income, or complex tax situations - Individuals experiencing liquidity events (sale, inheritance, settlement) - Families seeking a coordinated, single-advisor relationship > **Framing note:** Lead with **complexity and fit**, not "high-net-worth" labels or asset minimums. --- ## Part 3: Service Pillars These six pillars define the scope of JCSB's personal wealth advisory practice. Its power comes from its rarity. The rules below apply universally; medium-specific guides (Print, Web) layer additional permissions on top. ### Orange is always appropriate for: - Risk, urgency, deadline, or compliance-related language and borders - Structural emphasis that must immediately command attention - The highest-priority interactive element on a given page or panel (web: primary CTA button) ### Orange is never appropriate for: - General body text or paragraph emphasis - Decorative purposes without a content rationale - Competing signal roles — only one orange element per visual zone ### Medium-specific permissions: - **Print:** Structural and urgency-specific only. Left-edge panel rule, tension block border, urgency eyebrow labels on dark panels. See Print Design Guide for full policy. - **Web:** Broader use permitted — also used as the primary CTA button (nav, form submit) and in pain point block borders. See Web Design Guide for full policy. --- ## Part 7: Typography System ### Typefaces | Family | Role | Personality | |---|---|---| | **Playfair Display** | Headlines, display type, italic accents | Editorial authority — the voice of gravitas and refinement | | **DM Sans** | Body copy, labels, UI elements | Clean utility — the voice of clarity and precision | These two typefaces in combination are the visual engine of the brand. Warm. Credentialed. Local.** ### Tone Principles - **Lead with the problem, then the solution.** Clients respond to recognition of their pain before they respond to claims of excellence. - **Be direct, not dense.** Say it plainly. Financial services writing defaults to jargon as a credibility signal — JCSB's credibility is established by the CFP® designation and the quality of the reasoning, not by vocabulary. - **Sound human and local.** "We're not a 1-800 number" is both a fact and a tone direction. Write as if Brent is in the room. - **Avoid certainty promises.** No return guarantees, no promises about outcomes. Express confidence in the process, not the result. - **Use jargon only when it helps.** Technical depth (IRMAA, RMDs, cash flow modeling, 3(21) fiduciary) is a differentiator when deployed precisely — not when scattered throughout as ambient credentialing. ### Vocabulary — Master Use/Avoid Table This is the canonical vocabulary reference. The CFP® designation is the credential that separates JCSB from "representatives." - **No return guarantees.** Express confidence in process and planning, not outcomes. - **Disclose insurance commissions clearly** when relevant to the piece.
